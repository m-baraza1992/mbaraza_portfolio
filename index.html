About

Ten years reading the ground, five years mapping it.

I'm a GIS Officer at Mombasa Water Supply & Sanitation Co. Ltd (MOWASSCO), where I sit at the intersection of field survey work and spatial data engineering. Over ten years in the water and sanitation sector — the last five focused squarely on GIS — I've moved from reading pipe networks on the ground to building the systems that track them digitally.

My day-to-day runs on ArcGIS Enterprise and Python: pulling live meter and billing data from our ERP, building non-revenue water dashboards that correlate commercial losses with physical leaks, and running field surveys — from GPR pipeline detection to KoboToolbox-based structural condition assessments.
