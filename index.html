About

Bridging Field Data and Spatial Analytics Since 2016.

I'm a GIS Officer at Mombasa Water Supply & Sanitation Co. Ltd (MOWASSCO), where I sit at the intersection of field survey work and spatial data engineering. Over ten years in the water and sanitation sector, I've moved from reading pipe networks on the ground to building the systems that track them digitally.

My work spans the full GIS workflow, from field data collection and spatial analysis to building dashboards and systems that turn raw data into something decision-makers can actually use. I work primarily in ArcGIS Enterprise and Python, alongside tools like QGIS, and KoboToolbox, depending on what a project calls for.
